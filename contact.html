Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the quick answers most homeowners ask before contacting our customer desk.

How does the 15,000 Naira per month pay-as-you-go plan work?

Instead of paying millions upfront for a complete solar installation, you start with a modest installation down payment. After setup, you pay a simple fixed monthly utility subscription starting at 15,000 Naira per month. It replaces your daily petrol purchases with a calm, predictable utility budget.

How quickly can your engineering team install my solar setup?

Once your home site audit is confirmed and your setup order is locked in, our installation engineers complete the full setup within 24 to 48 hours across our primary hub regions.

Is the home site inspection completely free?

Yes. Our technician site assessment and energy sizing check are 100% free when you submit your consultation form or contact us through WhatsApp.

What happens if public power grid crashes or there is heavy rain?

Our high-efficiency glass solar panels gather power even on cloudy days, while our heavy-duty deep-cycle lithium storage retains enough power to run your household lights, fans, fridge, and TVs uninterrupted through total grid collapses.
