Official Sunrise Solar Privacy Terms

Your Privacy & Data Security Matter To Us

We believe in complete honesty and transparency. This policy clearly explains how Sunrise Solar collects, protects, and handles your information when you apply for or subscribe to our solar power services.

Last Updated: January 2026 Applies to All Pay-As-You-Go Subscriptions
1

Overview & Principles

Sunrise Solar ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. When you request a quote, apply for our 15,000 Naira monthly pay-as-you-go system, or schedule a home installation, you trust us with your personal details. We hold ourselves to strict privacy standards to keep your data safe at all times.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Every piece of information we collect is used solely to deliver reliable solar power to your home and keep your subscription running smoothly.

2

Information We Collect

To set up your home solar hardware and manage your pay-as-you-go account, we collect the following categories of information:

  • • Contact Information: Your full name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, and email address for customer communication and support.
  • • Installation Address: Your physical residential or business address to conduct site audits and install panels, battery units, and inverters.
  • • Billing & Subscription Details: Transaction records, monthly payment logs, and initial down payment records managed via authorized payment channels.
  • • Hardware Telemetry: System performance metrics, battery charge states, and power output details to monitor grid protection and catch hardware faults early.
3

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to provide continuous, high-quality energy service. Specifically, we use your data to:

Account Setup & Billing

Process your low down payment and handle your monthly pay-as-you-go subscriptions.

Technical Maintenance

Dispatch engineers for health checks, panel maintenance, and surge guard inspections.

System Health Monitoring

Remotely monitor inverter health to prevent unexpected power drops during grid collapse.

Customer Support

Respond directly to your support calls, WhatsApp inquiries, and energy questions.

4

Data Protection & Storage

We use industry-standard encryption protocols and secure databases to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

Access to your contact details and installation records is restricted to authorized Sunrise Solar personnel and certified field engineers who require access to complete installation or maintenance tasks.

5

Third-Party Service Providers

We only share necessary data with trusted third-party service partners under strict confidentiality agreements. These include:

  • • Licensed Payment Gateways: To securely process credit/debit card transactions and automated subscription debits.
  • • Installation Logistics Partners: Certified regional technicians who deliver heavy solar components to your property.
6

Your Data Privacy Rights

You have full control over your personal information. As a Sunrise Solar customer, you have the right to:

Request Access: Ask for a copy of all personal records we hold for your solar account.

Request Correction: Update your address, contact number, or phone details at any time.

Request Account Closure: Upon completing or ending your pay-as-you-go agreement, you may request complete deletion of your records subject to legal retention requirements.
