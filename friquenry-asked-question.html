Yes. Sunrise Solar uses deep-cycle lithium storage designed specifically to retain high electrical capacity for long night hours. Your lights, ceiling fans, smart TVs, decoders, and domestic refrigerators will stay steadily powered while your family sleeps in cool comfort.

Our intelligent instant-switch inverter monitors your home power second by second. The exact moment public electricity fails, it switches to clean solar power in less than 10 milliseconds. Your TV stays on, your decoder stays connected, and your lights never flicker.
